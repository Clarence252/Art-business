Sketching legacy, one line at a time

Namudoto Clarence founded LEGACY SKETCH WORKS after years of honing a distinctive style — minimalist yet profoundly expressive. Based between Kampala and Europe, his works are held in private collections worldwide. Every stroke is deliberate, every sketch a conversation between tradition and contemporary silence.

"I draw not what I see, but what lingers beneath the surface. My art is an archive of unspoken stories." — N. Clarence
