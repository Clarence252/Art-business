Frequently Asked Questions
How long does a commissioned piece take?
Typical turnaround time is 3 to 4 days depending on complexity and size. Rush orders may be available for an additional fee.
What are your price ranges?
Original sketches start at shs.100,000, detailed portraits from shs.150,000, and large-scale works vary. Contact me for a personalized quote.
Do you ship internationally?
No, I don't ship worldwide. Due to the shipping costs that vary by location, if such a case email me or whatsapp me for more information
Can I request revisions?
Absolutely. You'll receive a digital sketch for approval, and I offer up to two rounds of revisions to ensure you're completely satisfied.

Art of Intentionality

Namudoto Clarence explores the space between minimalist form and profound emotional depth. Based between Kampala and Europe, his work is defined by a deliberate, high-contrast aesthetic that captures what remains unseen.
